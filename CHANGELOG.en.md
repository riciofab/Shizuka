# Changelog

The English release notes. The app shows them as "What's new" on every TV that is not set to French;
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) holds the French ones. Each release needs its section in both files.

## 1.5.0

- **Organise the home screen from your phone.** In **Settings → Apps row → Categories → Organize from your phone**, scan the QR code: type your categories' names, reorder them and their apps, and move an app from one category to another. The home screen updates as soon as you save.
- **Recent row.** The apps you opened last, first on the home screen. Turn it on in **Settings → Apps row → Categories → Recent apps row**.
- **Drawers in the right order.** App drawers and **Continue Watching** show what you watched last first.
- **Shizuka speaks your language.** French is now complete, Portuguese (Portugal) and Japanese are new, and the release notes follow the TV's language. Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Arabic and Turkish were too incomplete and give way to English.
- **A reliable inputs bar.** It follows the TV's HDMI ports: an input plugged in later shows up by itself, **Add an input** offers the TV's ports, and icons are guessed better.
- **Photo frame at night.** Turn on **Dim the photo frame at night** for a much darker photo frame from sunset to sunrise.
- **Your TV at a glance.** **Updates & About** shows the TV's model, its Android version, its network address and its free storage.
- **Fixes.** The QR code for sending from a phone shows whole, moving an app saves its order to the right category, and the animated glow settles smoothly.

## 1.4.1

- **Hold Back for photo frame.** A short press on **Back** on the home screen no longer starts photo-frame mode: hold **Back** to show it.
- **A much lighter home screen.** The animated glow on the selected tile settles after 10 s without the remote and starts again on the next press: the idle home screen goes from about 60% to 2% of a processor core.
- **A lighter photo frame.** The Ken Burns effect and the OLED burn-in shift use half the resources, with no visible difference.
- **Release notes fixed.** What's new no longer shows old Plume Launcher notes.

## 1.4.0

- **Pin and manage drawer thumbnails.** Hold OK on any drawer thumbnail (TV channels, Continue Watching) to pin it first with a star (★), hide it, or open its app.
- **Photo frame slideshow.** A new setting cycles through your wallpapers with a soft cross-fade in photo-frame mode (every 5, 10 or 30 min).
- **Now playing in photo frame.** The title and artist of the music that is playing show under the clock in photo-frame mode, and follow the OLED shift.
- **Update download progress.** The percentage shows in real time while an update downloads.
- **HDMI detection and APK size.** Only the TV's real HDMI ports are listed, with no placeholder inputs, and the R8-optimised APK is under 1 MB.

## 1.3.0

- **Per-app drawer settings and 5 thumbnail sizes.** For each app, turn its drawer on or off and set its opening delay (0.5 s to 3.0 s), its feeds and its thumbnail size (**Smallest**, **Small**, **Standard**, **Large**, **Largest**). Free TV / OQEE starts at **Smallest**.
- **Better drawers.** Thumbnails sit closer under their apps row, **Back** closes the drawer without starting photo-frame mode, and the glow is never cut off at the top.
- **A finer photo frame.** The weather stays on screen, a soft borderless halo keeps the clock readable, photo wallpapers get a smooth **Ken Burns** effect, and **OLED Pixel Shift** protects the panel.

## 1.2.0

- **Expanded weather.** Select the weather under the clock to open a frosted-glass card with the city, the temperature, today's low and high, humidity, wind and a 3-day forecast.
- **Floating settings panel.** Settings now open as a floating glass card with four rounded corners and smooth open and close animations.
- **New 4K wallpapers.** A collection of Christmas wallpapers, downloaded straight from the Shizuka repository.
- **Better navigation.** Up and Down move in order between the top bar, the weather and the apps rows; the first tile on the left is no longer clipped, and drawer thumbnails load more reliably.

## 1.1.0

- **App drawers.** Select a video app (YouTube, SmartTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Crunchyroll, ADN, Shadowz, RTP Play, OQEE by Free) to unfold its feeds and Continue Watching with a smooth cascade animation.
- **Feed picker and delay.** Choose each app's feeds from its hold menu (**Drawer feed**) or from settings, and set the drawer's opening delay from **0.5 s to 3.0 s**.
- **OQEE by Free and RTP Play.** The channels you last watched in OQEE show in its drawer and open directly, and RTP Play feeds are fully supported.
- **Full-screen clock and weather in photo frame.** In photo-frame mode, the clock, the date and the weather grow and glide to the centre of the screen.
- **Category title alignment.** Category and Continue Watching titles follow the alignment chosen for the rows (Left, Centre, Right).

## 1.0.0

- **A fork of Plume.** Shizuka is a modern, smooth fork of Plume Launcher for Android TV.
- **Custom selection glow.** Eight neon themes (Google, Aurora, Sunset, Ocean, Fire, Cyberpunk, Pure white, Wallpaper colour), with an animation (spin, pulse or static) and an adjustable thickness.
- **New clocks and weather.** Pixel (2 lines) and modern Apple TV clock styles, with clean vector weather icons.
- **Depth.** Soft shadows under the selected tiles.
- **Categories and rows.** Create and reorder your own rows of apps, above or below Continue Watching.
- **A richer hold menu.** Hide an app straight from its hold menu.
- **Automatic photo frame.** After a delay you choose, the tiles hide and leave the wallpaper, the clock and the weather.
